No. F. 19(172)/Tpt/Ops/2019/ - State Compounding of Offences under Section 200 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988

Schedule of Compounding Fees for Specified Road Traffic Offences in Delhi
In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 200 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the Lieutenant Governor of the National Capital Territory of Delhi authorizes designated officers of the Delhi Traffic Police (Assistant Sub-Inspector and above) and Transport Department (Enforcement Officer and above) to compound the offences specified below at the rates indicated:

Section 177: General offences (First offence compounding fee: Rs. 500; Second or subsequent offence: Rs. 1,500)
Section 177A: Violation of mandatory road signs and regulations (Compounding fee: Rs. 500)
Section 178(3)(a): Refusal to ply contract carriage or auto-rickshaw (Compounding fee: Rs. 500)
Section 179(1): Disobedience of lawful orders or refusal of information (Compounding fee: Rs. 2,000)
Section 180: Allowing unauthorized persons without licence to drive (Compounding fee: Rs. 5,000)
Section 181: Driving without holding a valid driving licence (Compounding fee: Rs. 5,000)
Section 182(1): Applying for licence while disqualified (Compounding fee: Rs. 10,000)
Section 183(1): Over-speeding in light motor vehicle (First offence: Rs. 2,000; Medium/Heavy vehicle: Rs. 4,000)
Section 194B(1): Driving without wearing seatbelt (Compounding fee: Rs. 1,000)
Section 194C: Violation of safety measures on motorcycles / triple riding (Compounding fee: Rs. 1,000)
Section 194D: Riding two-wheeler without protective helmet (Compounding fee: Rs. 1,000)
Section 194F: Unreasonable blowing of horns / use of pressure horns (First offence: Rs. 1,000; Subsequent: Rs. 2,000)

Compounding Procedures and Dispute Resolution
A motorist issued an e-challan for a compoundable offence may pay the compounding fee within 90 days via the Delhi Traffic Police portal or national Parivahan e-challan gateway. If the compounding fee is not paid within the stipulated period, the violation is referred to the Virtual Court (Delhi) for judicial trial under Section 208 of the Act.
