MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 15th February, 2022
G.S.R. 136(E) - Central Motor Vehicles (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022 (Safety of Children on Motorcycles)

Rule 138(7): Safety Harness and Protective Headgear for Children
For children below the age of four years being carried as pillion riders on a two-wheeled motorcycle:
(a) The child shall wear a crash helmet conforming to the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications or a specialized crash helmet designed specifically for children;
(b) The driver shall ensure that the child is secured to the driver with an approved safety harness. The safety harness shall be lightweight, waterproof, cushioned, and possess a certified minimum tensile breaking strength of thirty kilograms;
(c) The speed of the motorcycle carrying a child below four years shall not exceed forty kilometres per hour (40 km/h).

Section 194C and Section 194D: Statutory Penalties for Child Safety Violations
Contravention of child safety rules while riding a motorcycle is punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000) and disqualification for holding a driving licence for a period of three months under Section 194C and Section 194D of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
