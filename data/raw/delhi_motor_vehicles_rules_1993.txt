GOVERNMENT OF NATIONAL CAPITAL TERRITORY OF DELHI
TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
DELHI MOTOR VEHICLES RULES, 1993 (DMVR)
State Statutory Rules governing traffic flow, public service vehicles, auto-rickshaw metering, and local transport permits in Delhi

Rule 6: Display of Badges by Public Service Vehicle Drivers
Every driver of a public service vehicle, stage carriage bus, or contract carriage auto-rickshaw in Delhi shall display an authorized metal or digital badge issued by the licensing authority, engraved with the driver's registered serial number and photograph.

Rule 11: Auto-Rickshaw Fare Meters and Passenger Carriage
Every auto-rickshaw and motor cab operating within the National Capital Territory of Delhi shall be fitted with a tamper-proof electronic fare meter calibrated according to the rates notified by the Government of NCT of Delhi. Refusal to ply or demanding fares in excess of the digital meter reading constitutes a violation punishable under Section 178 of the Act.

Rule 53: Clean Fuel (CNG/EV) Mandate for Public Transport in Delhi
All commercial passenger transport vehicles, including city buses, taxis, and auto-rickshaws registered in Delhi, shall operate exclusively on clean alternative fuels such as Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) or battery electric propulsion. Entry of non-compliant commercial diesel vehicles into notified low-emission zones is strictly prohibited.
