THE MOTOR VEHICLES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019
NO. 32 OF 2019
MINISTRY OF LAW AND JUSTICE (LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT)
New Delhi, the 9th August, 2019 / Sravana 18, 1941 (Saka)
An Act further to amend the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

CHAPTER XIII: OFFENCES, PENALTIES AND PROCEDURE

Section 177: General provision for punishment of offences
Whoever contravenes any provision of this Act or of any rule, regulation or notification made thereunder shall, if no penalty is provided for the offence under this Act, be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees (Rs. 500);
(b) For any second or subsequent offence, with fine which may extend to one thousand five hundred rupees (Rs. 1,500).

Section 177A: Penalty for contravention of regulations made under Section 118
Whoever contravenes the regulations such as lane driving regulations, violation of traffic signals, or rules of the road regulations made under Section 118 shall be punishable with fine which shall not be less than five hundred rupees (Rs. 500) but which may extend to one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000).

Section 178: Penalty for travelling without pass or ticket and for dereliction of duty on the part of conductor and refusal to ply contract carriage
(1) Whoever travels in a stage carriage without having a proper pass or ticket with him or not being produced shall be punishable with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees (Rs. 500).
(3) If the holder of a permit or driver of a contract carriage refuses to ply the contract carriage or carry the agreed passengers, they shall be punishable with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees (Rs. 500).

Section 179: Disobedience of orders, obstruction and refusal of information
(1) Whoever wilfully disobeys any direction lawfully given by any person or authority empowered under this Act, or obstructs any person or authority in the discharge of their functions, shall be punishable with fine which may extend to two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000).
(2) Whoever, being required by or under this Act to supply any information, withholds such information or gives information which he knows to be false, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one month, or with fine which may extend to two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000), or with both.

Section 180: Allowing unauthorized persons to drive vehicles
Whoever, being the owner or person in charge of a motor vehicle, causes or permits any other person who does not satisfy the provisions of Section 3 or Section 4 to drive the vehicle, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine of five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000), or with both.

Section 181: Driving vehicles in contravention of Section 3 or Section 4 (Driving without a valid Driving Licence or underage driving)
Whoever drives a motor vehicle in contravention of Section 3 (necessity for driving licence) or Section 4 (age limit in connection with driving of motor vehicles) shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine of five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000), or with both.

Section 182: Offences relating to licences
Whoever drives a motor vehicle while disqualified from holding or obtaining a driving licence, or obtains or applies for a licence while disqualified, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both.

Section 182A: Punishment for offences relating to construction, maintenance, and sale of motor vehicles
Any person who alters a motor vehicle, including parts or components, in contravention of Chapter VII, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine of one lakh rupees (Rs. 1,00,000) per vehicle.

Section 183: Driving at excessive speed (Speeding)
(1) Whoever drives or causes any person who is employed by him to drive a motor vehicle in contravention of the speed limits referred to in Section 112 shall be punishable:
(i) In the case of a Light Motor Vehicle (LMV), with fine which shall not be less than one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000) and not more than two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000);
(ii) In the case of a medium goods vehicle, medium passenger vehicle, heavy goods vehicle or heavy passenger motor vehicle, with fine which shall not be less than two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000) and not more than four thousand rupees (Rs. 4,000);
(2) For a second or subsequent offence under this section, the driving licence of the driver may be impounded.

Section 184: Driving dangerously (Dangerous Driving / Rash Driving / Red Light Jumping / Handheld Mobile Use while driving)
Whoever drives a motor vehicle at a speed or in a manner which is dangerous to the public, having regard to all the circumstances of the case, including jumping a red light, violating stop signs, using handheld communications devices (mobile phones) while driving, or passing or overtaking other vehicles in a manner contrary to law, shall be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine which shall not be less than one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000) but may extend to five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000), or with both;
(b) For any second or subsequent offence committed within three years of the previous offence, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both.

Section 185: Driving by a drunken person or by a person under the influence of drugs (Drunk Driving)
Whoever, while driving or attempting to drive a motor vehicle:
(a) Has in his blood, alcohol exceeding 30 mg per 100 ml of blood detected in a test by a breath analyser; or
(b) Is under the influence of a drug to such an extent as to be incapable of exercising proper control over the vehicle,
shall be punishable:
(i) For the first offence, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both;
(ii) For any second or subsequent offence committed within three years, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine of fifteen thousand rupees (Rs. 15,000), or with both.

Section 186: Driving when mentally or physically unfit to drive
Whoever drives a motor vehicle in any public place when he is to his knowledge suffering from any disease or disability calculated to cause his driving of the vehicle to be a source of danger to the public, shall be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000);
(b) For any second or subsequent offence, with fine which may extend to two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000).

Section 189: Racing and trials of speed
Whoever without the written consent of the State Government permits or takes part in a race or trial of speed between motor vehicles in any public place shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine of five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000), or with both. For a second or subsequent offence, imprisonment up to one year, or fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or both.

Section 190: Using vehicle in unsafe condition
(1) Any person who drives or causes or allows to be driven in any public place a motor vehicle or trailer, which has any defect which causes or is likely to cause danger to the public, shall be punishable with fine of one thousand five hundred rupees (Rs. 1,500).
(2) Violation of standards relating to road safety, noise, or air pollution (No PUC certificate): punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine of up to ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both, and licence disqualification for three months.

Section 192: Using vehicle without registration (Unregistered Vehicle)
Whoever drives or causes or allows to be driven a motor vehicle without registration shall be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with a fine which shall not be less than two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000) but may extend to five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000);
(b) For any second or subsequent offence, with imprisonment which may extend to one year, or with fine which shall not be less than five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000) but may extend to ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both.

Section 192A: Using vehicle without permit (Commercial vehicles)
Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be used in contravention of the provisions of sub-section (1) of Section 66 or in contravention of any condition of a permit shall be punishable:
For the first offence with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, and with fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000);
For a subsequent offence with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year, and with fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000).

Section 194: Driving vehicle exceeding permissible weight (Overloading of Goods Vehicles)
Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of Section 113 or Section 114 or Section 115 (overloading) shall be punishable:
With a minimum fine of twenty thousand rupees (Rs. 20,000), and an additional fine of two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000) per tonne of excess load, together with the liability to pay charges for off-loading the excess weight.

Section 194A: Carriage of excess passengers (Overloading in Passenger Vehicles)
Whoever drives a transport vehicle or causes or allows a transport vehicle to be driven while carrying more passengers than is permissible shall be punishable:
With a fine of two hundred rupees (Rs. 200) for each excess passenger.

Section 194B: Use of safety belts and seating of children (Seatbelt Violation)
(1) Whoever drives a motor vehicle without wearing a safety belt or carries passengers not wearing seat belts shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000).
(2) Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be driven with a child who, not having attained the age of fourteen years, is not secured by a safety belt or child restraint system, shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000).

Section 194C: Penalty for violation of safety measures for motorcycle drivers and pillion riders (Helmet Violation and Triple Riding)
Whoever drives a motorcycle or causes or allows a motorcycle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of Section 128 (carrying more than one person in addition to the driver / triple riding) or Section 129 (riding without protective headgear conforming to standards / without helmet) shall be punishable:
With a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000) and the driver shall be disqualified for holding a driving licence for a period of three months.

Section 194D: Penalty for not wearing protective headgear (No Helmet)
Whoever drives a motorcycle or causes or allows a motorcycle to be driven without wearing protective headgear (helmet) conforming to the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) shall be punishable with a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000) and disqualification of the driving licence for three months.

Section 194E: Failure to allow free passage to emergency vehicles (Ambulance, Fire Brigade)
Whoever while driving a motor vehicle fails to draw to the side of the roadway, on the approach of a fire service vehicle or of an ambulance or other emergency vehicle as designated, shall be punishable:
With imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with a fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both.

Section 194F: Use of horns and silence zones
Whoever uses any vehicle horn needlessly or continuously or in a silence zone, or drives a vehicle which is fitted with any multi-toned horn or unauthorized siren, shall be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with a fine of one thousand rupees (Rs. 1,000);
(b) For any second or subsequent offence, with a fine of two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000).

Section 196: Driving uninsured vehicle (No Motor Insurance)
Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of Section 146 (necessity for insurance against third party risk) shall be punishable:
(a) For the first offence, with imprisonment which may extend to three months, or with fine of two thousand rupees (Rs. 2,000), or with both;
(b) For any subsequent offence, with imprisonment which may extend to three months, or with fine of four thousand rupees (Rs. 4,000), or with both.

Section 199A: Offences by juveniles (Underage Driving by Minor)
(1) Where an offence under this Act has been committed by a juvenile, the guardian of such juvenile or the owner of the motor vehicle shall be deemed to be guilty of the offence and shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished:
Punishment: Imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years and with fine of twenty-five thousand rupees (Rs. 25,000).
Registration cancellation: The registration of the motor vehicle used in the offence shall be cancelled for a period of twelve months.
Juvenile eligibility: The juvenile shall not be eligible to be granted a driving licence under Section 9 or learner's licence under Section 8 until such juvenile has attained the age of twenty-five years.

Section 200: Composition of certain offences (Compounding of fines)
Any offence punishable under Section 177, 178, 179, 180, 181, 182, 183, 184, 186, 189, 190, 192, 194, 194A, 194B, 194C, 194D, 194E, 194F, 196 may, either before or after the institution of the prosecution, be compounded by such officers or authorities and for such amount as the State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, specify in this behalf.
