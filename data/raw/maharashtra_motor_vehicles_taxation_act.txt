GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
THE MAHARASHTRA MOTOR VEHICLES TAXATION ACT
(BOMBAY ACT NO. LXV OF 1958 / MAHARASHTRA ACT NO. LXV OF 1958)
State Statutory Enactment governing the levy and collection of motor vehicles road tax and passenger cess in the State of Maharashtra

Section 3: Levy of tax on motor vehicles in Maharashtra
A tax shall be leviable on all motor vehicles used or kept for use in the State of Maharashtra at the rates specified in the First Schedule. For non-transport passenger vehicles (cars and two-wheelers), one-time tax (OTT) is assessed on the registration cost, with concessional slabs for battery-operated electric vehicles (EVs).

Section 9: Refund of tax on vehicle removal or deregistration
Where a motor vehicle in respect of which tax has been paid is removed to any other State or Union Territory on permanent transfer, or where the registration of the vehicle is cancelled due to destruction or scrap, the registered owner shall be entitled to a pro-rata refund of the one-time tax for the unexpired statutory period.

Section 16: Recovery of arrears of tax and penalty
Any tax or penalty due under this Act may be recovered in the same manner as an arrear of land revenue. Transport enforcement officers are authorized to seize and detain vehicles operating without tax payment until the arrears together with statutory simple interest are fully discharged.
