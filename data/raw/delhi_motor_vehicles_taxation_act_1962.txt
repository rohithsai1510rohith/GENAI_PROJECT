GOVERNMENT OF NATIONAL CAPITAL TERRITORY OF DELHI
THE DELHI MOTOR VEHICLES TAXATION ACT, 1962
(ACT NO. 57 OF 1962)
State Enactment governing vehicular taxation, lifetime road tax, green cess, and tax defaults in the National Capital Territory of Delhi

Section 3: Levy of road tax on motor vehicles
Subject to the other provisions of this Act, there shall be levied and collected on all motor vehicles used or kept for use in Delhi a tax at the rates specified in the First Schedule to this Act. One-time road tax is payable at the time of initial vehicle registration based on vehicle invoice price, fuel type, and engine displacement.

Section 3A: Environment Compensation Charge and Green Cess
In addition to the road tax levied under Section 3, an Environment Compensation Charge or Green Tax shall be levied on transport and commercial diesel vehicles entering or operating within the National Capital Territory of Delhi to mitigate air pollution and support sustainable public transport initiatives.

Section 13: Penalties for non-payment of road tax
Whoever fails to pay the motor vehicle road tax within the statutory grace period of thirty days shall be liable to pay a penalty at the rate of two per cent per month on the overdue tax amount, and the vehicle is liable to be impounded by the transport department or enforcement wing until full tax and penalty clearance is produced.
