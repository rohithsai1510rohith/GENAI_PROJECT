New Delhi, the 18th April, 2023
G.S.R. 302(E) - All India Tourist Vehicles (Permit) Rules, 2023

Rule 3: Application for All India Tourist Authorization and Permit
An application for grant of an All India Tourist Permit for a tourist vehicle shall be made electronically on the Parivahan portal to the designated State Transport Authority accompanied by the prescribed composite fee depending on seating capacity.

Rule 4: Scope and Validity of Permit
An All India Tourist Permit shall be valid throughout the territory of India without the requirement of obtaining separate individual state permits or paying local road taxes at state border checkposts. The permit shall be granted for a period of thirty days or one year or three years or five years at a time.

Rule 7: Conditions of Tourist Vehicle Operation
Every tourist vehicle operating under an All India Tourist Permit shall display the words 'All India Tourist Vehicle' on the front and rear of the vehicle in bold letters. The vehicle shall be equipped with a functional vehicle location tracking device (VLTD) and an emergency panic button compliant with AIS-140 standards.

Section 192A: Penalty for using vehicle without permit
Whoever drives a motor vehicle or causes or allows a motor vehicle to be used in contravention of the provisions of sub-section (1) of section 66 or in contravention of any condition of a permit shall be punishable for the first offence with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with a fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000), or with both.
