MOTOR VEHICLES (DRIVING) REGULATIONS, 2017
MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
NOTIFICATION G.S.R. 634(E), NEW DELHI, DATED 23RD JUNE, 2017
PUBLISHED IN THE GAZETTE OF INDIA, EXTRAORDINARY

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 118 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (59 of 1988), the Central Government hereby makes the Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017 to specify the statutory rules of the road and driver conduct throughout India:

Regulation 5 & 6: Lane Driving, Lane Changing and Keeping to the Left
(1) Every driver of a motor vehicle shall drive the vehicle as close to the left side of the road as is practicable, allowing oncoming traffic to pass on the right.
(2) On multi-lane roads, drivers shall adhere strictly to their designated lane. Lane changing without prior indicator signalling at least three seconds before the manoeuvre is an offence under Section 177A.
(3) The extreme right lane on divided carriageways is reserved exclusively for overtaking and emergency vehicles. Plying slowly in the rightmost lane is prohibited.

Regulation 11: Duty towards Pedestrians at Zebra Crossings
(1) Pedestrians have absolute right of way at all uncontrolled pedestrian crossings (zebra crossings).
(2) Every driver approaching an uncontrolled pedestrian crossing shall slow down and come to a complete stop before the stop line to allow pedestrians to cross.
(3) Stopping or parking a motor vehicle on or within five metres of a pedestrian crossing is strictly prohibited. Violation is punishable under Section 177 and Section 184.

Regulation 14: Overtaking Regulations
(1) The driver of a motor vehicle shall overtake other vehicles only on the right side.
(2) Overtaking on the left is permitted only when the vehicle being overtaken is signalling to turn right.
(3) Overtaking is strictly prohibited:
(a) At pedestrian crossings, intersections, and railway level crossings;
(b) On blind bends, hill crests, or narrow bridges;
(c) Where the road is marked with a solid continuous white or yellow line.

Regulation 16: Roundabout Navigation and Right of Way
(1) When entering a roundabout, drivers shall yield right of way to vehicles already circulating on the roundabout from their right.
(2) Drivers shall signal their intention before entering and exiting the roundabout.

Regulation 22: Stopping and Parking Restrictions
(1) No vehicle shall be parked or brought to a halt:
(a) On or near a blind curve, crest of a hill, or opposite another standing vehicle;
(b) On footpaths, cycle tracks, or designated pedestrian paths;
(c) Within ten metres of a bus stop, traffic signal, or junction;
(d) On highways except in designated lay-by bays or emergency stopping lanes.
(2) Any vehicle obstructing the free flow of traffic may be towed away and the driver penalized under Section 122 and Section 177.

Regulation 27: Duty to Give Free Passage to Emergency Vehicles
(1) On the approach of an emergency vehicle (fire service tender, ambulance, or police escort) sounding an auditory siren or displaying flashing emergency beacons, every driver shall immediately draw their vehicle to the extreme left and stop.
(2) Tailing or closely following an emergency vehicle within fifty metres is prohibited. Failure to yield free passage is an offence under Section 194E with mandatory fine of ten thousand rupees (Rs. 10,000).

Regulation 30: Use of Vehicle Horns and Silence Zones
(1) Drivers shall sound the horn only when necessary for safety to warn other road users of imminent danger.
(2) Sounding horn in designated silence zones (hospitals, schools, courts) or fitting multi-toned or pressure horns is strictly prohibited under Section 194F.
