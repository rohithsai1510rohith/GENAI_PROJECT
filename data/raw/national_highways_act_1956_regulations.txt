THE NATIONAL HIGHWAYS ACT, 1956
(ACT NO. 48 OF 1956)
Statutory provisions governing National Highway declaration, land access, toll collections, and highway traffic regulations

Section 4: National highways to vest in the Union
All national highways shall vest in the Union, and for the purposes of this Act "highways" include all lands appurtenant thereto, whether demarcated or not; all bridges, culverts, tunnels, causeways, carriageways and other structures constructed on or across such highways.

Section 8A: Power of Central Government to enter into agreements for development and maintenance
The Central Government may enter into an agreement with any person in relation to the development and maintenance of the whole or any part of a national highway, including collection of fee for services or benefits rendered on such highway (FASTag and electronic toll collection).

Section 24 of Control of National Highways (Land and Traffic) Act, 2002: Prevention of unauthorized highway access and encroachment
No person shall occupy any highway land or discharge any trade effluent or obstruct highway traffic without permission. Unauthorized structures or barricades on National Highways are subject to immediate demolition and statutory removal with costs assessed against the encroacher.
