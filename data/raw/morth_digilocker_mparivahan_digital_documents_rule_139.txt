MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 17th December, 2018 / 1st October, 2020
Rule 139 and Rule 139A: Production of Licence, Certificate of Registration and Other Documents in Electronic Form

Rule 139: Production of licence and certificate of registration
The driver or a person in charge of a motor vehicle shall produce the certificate of registration, insurance, fitness and permit, the driving licence and any other relevant documents on demand by any police officer in uniform or any other officer authorized by the State Government in this behalf.
Provided that if any or all of the documents are not in his possession at the time of demand, the person shall either produce them in electronic form through the DigiLocker platform or the mParivahan mobile application, which shall be accepted as valid proof of the documents under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (21 of 2000) and the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989.

Rule 139A: Time limit for production of documents
If the documents are not produced immediately in electronic or physical form at the time of inspection, the driver or owner of the motor vehicle shall produce the certificate of registration, insurance, fitness, permit, and driving licence in physical or electronic form within seven (7) days from the date of demand before the police officer or the authorized transport official at the concerned police station or office of the licensing authority.

Section 4 of the Information Technology Act, 2000: Legal recognition of electronic records
Where any law provides that information or any other matter shall be in writing or in the typewritten or printed form, then, notwithstanding anything contained in such law, such requirement shall be deemed to have been satisfied if such information or matter is rendered or made available in an electronic form and accessible so as to be usable for a subsequent reference. Physical seizure of documents is strictly prohibited when valid electronic documents are made available on DigiLocker or mParivahan.
