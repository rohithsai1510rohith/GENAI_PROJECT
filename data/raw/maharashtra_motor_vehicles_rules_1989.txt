GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
HOME DEPARTMENT (TRANSPORT)
MAHARASHTRA MOTOR VEHICLES RULES, 1989 (MMVR)
State Statutory Rules governing regional transport authorities, stage carriages, school buses, and road regulations across Maharashtra

Rule 24: Special Safety Regulations for School Buses in Maharashtra
Every motor vehicle used for the carriage of school children in Maharashtra shall be painted in highway golden yellow colour with the words 'School Bus' prominently written on the front and rear. The bus shall be fitted with horizontal window grills, emergency exit doors with alarm buzzers, speed governors calibrated to 40 km/h, GPS location trackers, and CCTV cameras. A trained female attendant shall be present on all trips carrying female pupils.

Rule 112: Carriage of Goods on Motor Vehicles
No motor vehicle shall carry goods exceeding the permissible laden weight recorded in the certificate of registration or projecting horizontally more than thirty centimetres beyond the outer edge of the tyres. Overhang beyond the rear body must display a red flag by day and a red light by night.

Rule 115: Noise and Decibel Limits for Vehicle Horns in Urban Areas
No motor vehicle in urban areas of Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, and other municipal corporations in Maharashtra shall be fitted with multi-toned, musical, or air pressure horns. Use of horns within declared Silence Zones (surrounding hospitals, educational institutions, and courts) is prohibited and subject to immediate compoundable fines.
