TAMIL NADU MOTOR VEHICLES RULES, 1989 (TNMVR)
State Statutory Rules governing omnibus operations, stage carriages, driver safety certifications, and road safety councils across Tamil Nadu

Rule 15: Special Conditions for State Passenger Stage Carriages
Every stage carriage operating in Tamil Nadu shall provide reserved seating for women, senior citizens, and persons with disabilities. Automatic pneumatic passenger doors or safety bar gates shall be maintained in working condition while the vehicle is in motion to prevent falls from footboards.

Rule 278: Tamil Nadu State Road Safety Council and District Road Safety Committees
The State Government constitutes the Tamil Nadu State Road Safety Council headed by the Minister for Transport, along with District Road Safety Committees chaired by District Collectors, to review accident fatality reduction targets, identify black spots on State Highways, and monitor road safety funds.

Rule 348: Prohibition of Audio and Visual Distractions in Transport Vehicles
Fitting of video playback screens or loudspeakers within the sight or immediate hearing range of the driver while operating transport buses or omnibuses is prohibited to prevent distraction. Violation constitutes an offence under Section 177 of the Act.
