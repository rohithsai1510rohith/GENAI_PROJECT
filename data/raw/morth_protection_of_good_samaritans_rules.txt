CENTRAL MOTOR VEHICLES (PROTECTION OF GOOD SAMARITANS) RULES
Notification G.S.R. 594(E) and Section 134A of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) | Gazette of India
Jurisdiction: Central (Applicable throughout India) | Effective Date: 2020-09-29

Section 134A: Protection of Good Samaritans
(1) A Good Samaritan shall not be liable for any civil or criminal action for any injury to or death of an accident victim caused due to their negligence in providing emergency medical or non-medical care or assistance.
(2) The Central Government may make rules regarding the examination and interrogation of a Good Samaritan, protecting them from discrimination, coercion, or harassment by police authorities or hospitals.

Rule 3: Rights and Protections of a Good Samaritan at Hospitals
(1) Any person who takes an injured road accident victim to a hospital or medical clinic shall be allowed to leave immediately after providing the victim's location or details if known, without answering unnecessary questions.
(2) No hospital, doctor, or medical establishment shall compel a Good Samaritan to provide their personal name, address, telephone number, relationship to the victim, or proof of identity.
(3) No hospital shall demand any registration fees or upfront monetary deposit from a Good Samaritan as a condition for initiating immediate medical treatment or emergency resuscitation of an injured person.
(4) Denial of immediate trauma or emergency medical treatment to an injured accident victim is a severe violation punishable under law.

Rule 4: Police Interrogation and Witness Procedures
(1) A Good Samaritan shall not be compelled to become a witness in any legal or criminal proceedings arising out of the accident.
(2) Where a Good Samaritan chooses voluntarily to give evidence as a witness, the examination by police authorities:
(a) Shall be conducted at a time and place of the Good Samaritan's convenience, such as their residence or place of business;
(b) The police officer conducting the examination shall not wear police uniform and shall conduct the inquiry in a civil and courteous manner;
(c) The statement may be recorded through video conferencing or digital electronic means if requested by the Good Samaritan.
(3) Under no circumstances shall a Good Samaritan be detained or kept overnight at any police station.

Rule 5: Cash Rewards and Recognition Scheme for Good Samaritans
(1) Under the MoRTH Good Samaritan Scheme (Notification No. RT-25035/101/2017-RS), any individual who saves the life of an accident victim by rushing them to a hospital within the Golden Hour (the first hour following trauma injury) shall be eligible for:
(a) A cash award of five thousand rupees (Rs. 5,000) per incident;
(b) A Certificate of Appreciation issued by the District Appraisal Committee.
(2) An individual may be recognized multiple times for distinct life-saving rescue acts. National annual awards of one lakh rupees (Rs. 1,00,000) are conferred upon the top ten Good Samaritans nationally.
